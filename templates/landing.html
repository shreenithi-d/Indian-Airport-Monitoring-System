Everything you need to know about this aviation platform

The application features 110 verified Indian airports with genuine geographical coordinates, official IATA and ICAO codes, airport categories (International, Domestic, Customs, Defense Civil Enclave), and operational statuses verified from civil aviation reference records.

The seat map provides an interactive top-down aircraft layout. Confirmed male passenger seats are visually indicated in light blue (#DCEBFF with border #4385E8), female passenger seats are indicated in light pink (#FFE0EC with border #E66B9A), available seats in green, and held or restricted seats with dedicated badges.
